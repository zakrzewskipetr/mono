No sound

MONO 01 respects the silent switch. Turn silent mode off and raise the volume. Your music keeps playing.

No vibration

Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics. iPad has no vibration motor, so there you hear the sound only.

Negative numbers

There is no ± key. Type the minus after an operator: 6 × − 7 = −42.

Where is %, history, settings?

Left out on purpose. One purpose, done down to the last detail.
